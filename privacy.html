Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

This application is a private tool used only by its owner to publish and manage the owner's own videos and posts on the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Threads and TikTok. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is accessed

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, read video and channel status, and manage live broadcasts on the owner's channels through the YouTube Data API.
  • Instagram and Threads (Meta): the owner's account ID and username, and permission to publish the owner's videos and captions and read their publishing status, through the Instagram API with Instagram Login and the Threads API.
  • TikTok: the owner's basic profile (open ID, display name) and permission to upload and publish the owner's videos through the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

How it is stored and used

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer and are used only to publish and manage the owner's own content. No data is collected from other people, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. Videos are made briefly available at a temporary link only so the platform can fetch them for publishing.

Platform policies

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy. Data from Meta is handled under the Meta Platform Terms and the Meta Privacy Policy. Data from TikTok is handled under the TikTok Developer Terms.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions, in Instagram or Threads settings under Apps and websites, or in TikTok settings under Security and permissions. To have stored tokens and data deleted, see Data Deletion.

Contact

Questions can be sent through this site's GitHub issues.
